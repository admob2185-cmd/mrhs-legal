Yearblink – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026 · App: Yearblink (com.yearblink.app) · Developer: MR and HS Devs

In short: Yearblink does not collect, send or share any personal data. Your videos, photos and notes stay on your phone. There is no account, no server, no cloud, no ads and no analytics – the app does not even request internet access.

1. What the app stores – only on your phone

  • Your clips: the 1-second videos you record, and copies of the videos or photos you pick from your gallery, together with small thumbnails. They are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Diary data: the date of each clip, its file path and the optional short note you write. This is kept in a local database on your phone.
  • Settings: app language, film options (format, date, notes, music choice) and whether you have finished the introduction.
  • Finished films: when you make a film, it is saved to your phone's gallery (Movies/Yearblink). From there it is managed by Android and your gallery app like any other video.

We (the developer) never receive any of this data. We have no servers.

2. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera – to record your daily second. Used only while the recording screen is open.
  • Microphone – to record sound with your clip. You can refuse it; clips are then recorded without sound.
  • Notifications – to show the progress of film export and to tell you when your film is ready.
  • Foreground service – so the film keeps being made when you leave the app. All processing happens on your phone.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only) – to save the finished film to the gallery. Newer Android versions need no storage permission.
  • Photos and videos from the gallery are chosen with the Android photo picker. The app gets access only to the items you select, never to your whole gallery.
  • Google Play billing – to offer the optional Pro purchase (see section 4).

3. Sharing

The app never shares anything by itself. When you tap “Share”, Android shows its share menu and the film goes only to the app you choose (for example TikTok, Instagram or WhatsApp). What happens to it there is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

4. Purchases (Pro)

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your name, e-mail address or payment details. The app only asks Google Play on your phone whether Pro is active. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

5. No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and no tracking. It does not use your location, contacts or advertising ID.

6. Backup

Your videos are not included in Android's automatic cloud backup. When you move to a new phone with Android's direct device-to-device transfer, Android may copy the app's data to your new phone. If you want to keep your clips, back them up yourself (for example by saving your films to the gallery).

7. Deleting your data

You can delete any clip in the app. Uninstalling the app (or Settings → Apps → Yearblink → Storage → Clear data) permanently deletes all clips, notes and settings. Films already saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them in your gallery app.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
