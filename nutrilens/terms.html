2. What the app does – and does not do

NutriLens scans barcodes of food and cosmetic products and rates them from 0 to 100 using data from the public databases Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts, personalised by the preferences you set (allergens, diet, goals).

The app is an information aid, not medical advice. The databases are maintained by volunteers and can be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Allergen warnings can be missing. Always read the product label, especially if you have an allergy or a medical condition, and consult a doctor or dietitian for medical decisions.

Vždy si prečítajte obal produktu, najmä ak máte alergiu alebo zdravotný problém, a pri zdravotných rozhodnutiach sa poraďte s lekárom alebo dietológom.
