Privacy Policy – Cubeloom

Effective date: 26 November 2026

1. Who we are

Cubeloom (Android package sk.dedik.cubeloom) is a block puzzle game developed and published by MR and HS Devs, the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact for all privacy questions: prvyucetjedna@gmail.com.

2. Summary

Cubeloom has no user accounts and we run no game server. We, the developer, do not receive or store your name, e-mail, contacts, location or payment details. Some data is processed by Google services built into the game (ads, purchases, optional analytics and cloud save), as described below.

3. Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, scores, coins, boosters, island, settings, daily streak and purchase status) is stored in the app's private storage on your device and may be included in your Android device backup. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob and consent (Google UMP)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): rewarded ads you choose to watch and, occasionally, ads between games (never during play). To show, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language), approximate location derived from IP, and ad interactions and diagnostics. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & ad choices. Without consent only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Google › Ads). Players who bought “No Ads” or have VIP do not see ads between games. More: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Purchases – Google Play Billing

In-app purchases and the VIP subscription are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The game receives only a purchase record (product, purchase token, status) from Google Play to unlock what you bought. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Analytics – Google Firebase

If enabled, the game uses Google Firebase Analytics (including basic crash and app-stability events) and Remote Config to understand how the game is played (e.g. which levels are too hard, how many players return, app crashes) and to deliver new levels and settings. This data is pseudonymous (an app instance ID; no name or e-mail) and is processed by Google on our behalf. Firebase data is kept for up to 14 months.

7. Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your progress can be saved to your Google account (Saved Games) and your scores may appear on leaderboards under your Play Games profile. This is optional; you can sign out at any time in the Play Games app.

8. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders (e.g. “Daily challenge is ready”) on your device. No data is sent for this.

9. Legal bases

Consent (personalised ads, analytics where required), performance of a contract (purchases), and legitimate interest (security, fraud prevention, basic ad delivery and app stability).

10. Data sharing and transfers

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the Google services listed above, which may process it outside the EU under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses / EU–US Data Privacy Framework. All data sent by the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

11. Children

Cubeloom is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Your rights and deletion

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Slovakia: Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR). Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, you can delete all game data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it; Google data can be managed at myaccount.google.com and by resetting your advertising ID. For any request write to prvyucetjedna@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

13. Changes

We may update this policy; the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Zásady ochrany súkromia – Cubeloom

Účinné od: 26. novembra 2026

1. Kto sme

Cubeloom (balík sk.dedik.cubeloom) je logická hra s blokmi od vývojára MR and HS Devs, prevádzkovateľa podľa GDPR. Kontakt: prvyucetjedna@gmail.com.

2. Zhrnutie

Hra nemá používateľské účty a nemáme vlastný server. Nedostávame tvoje meno, e-mail, kontakty, polohu ani platobné údaje. Niektoré údaje spracúvajú služby Google zabudované v hre (reklamy, nákupy, voliteľne analytika a cloudové ukladanie).

3. Údaje v zariadení

Postup v hre (levely, hviezdy, skóre, mince, pomôcky, ostrov, nastavenia, séria, stav nákupov) sa ukladá iba v súkromnom úložisku aplikácie a môže byť súčasťou zálohy Androidu. Odinštalovaním hry sa vymaže.

4. Reklamy – Google AdMob a súhlas (Google UMP)

Bezplatná verzia zobrazuje reklamy Google AdMob (videá za odmenu a občas reklamu medzi hrami, nikdy počas hrania). AdMob môže zhromažďovať reklamný identifikátor, IP adresu, údaje o zariadení a aplikácii, približnú polohu z IP a interakcie s reklamami. V EHP, Spojenom kráľovstve a Švajčiarsku si pred personalizovanými reklamami vyžiadame súhlas cez Google UMP; zmeniť ho môžeš v Nastavenia › Súkromie a reklamy.

5. Nákupy – Google Play

Nákupy a predplatné VIP spracúva výhradne Google Play. Platobné údaje nevidíme; hra dostane len záznam o nákupe. Predplatné zrušíš v Google Play › Platby a predplatné.

6. Analytika – Firebase

Ak je zapnutá, hra používa Google Firebase Analytics a Remote Config na pseudonymné štatistiky hrania, diagnostiku pádov a doručenie nových levelov. Údaje sa uchovávajú najviac 14 mesiacov.

7. Google Play Hry (voliteľné)

Po prihlásení do Google Play Hier sa môže postup ukladať do tvojho účtu Google a skóre sa môže zobraziť v rebríčkoch.

8. Deti

Hra nie je určená deťom do 13 rokov.

9. Tvoje práva a vymazanie

Máš právo na prístup, opravu, vymazanie, obmedzenie, námietku, prenosnosť, odvolanie súhlasu a sťažnosť na Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR. Všetky údaje hry vymažeš vymazaním dát aplikácie alebo odinštalovaním; údaje Google spravuješ na myaccount.google.com. Žiadosti posielaj na prvyucetjedna@gmail.com. Údaje sa prenášajú šifrovane (HTTPS). Údaje nepredávame.
