Privacy Policy – Dice of Fate

Effective date: 26 November 2026

1. Who we are

Dice of Fate (Android package com.mrandhsdevs.diceoffate) is a single-player dice game developed and published by MR and HS Devs, the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact for all privacy questions: prvyucetjedna@gmail.com.

2. Summary

Dice of Fate has no user accounts and no game server. We, the developer, do not collect, receive or store any personal data about you.

Your game progress (runs, unlocks, currencies, settings and purchase records) is stored only locally on your device.

The free version shows advertising from Google AdMob, and purchases are processed by Google Play. These Google services process some data as described below.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves its progress and settings in the app's private storage on your phone. To complete purchases reliably, it also stores the Google Play purchase tokens of your purchases there. This data never leaves your device through us; we have no access to it. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage or by uninstalling the app (in-game: Settings › Reset all progress deletes the game progress).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Dice of Fate shows full-screen (interstitial) ads after some finished runs, using Google AdMob provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. To deliver, measure and prevent fraud in ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process: the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AD_ID), device information (model, operating system version, language, screen), IP address and approximate location derived from it, app information, and interactions with ads (views, clicks).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings › Privacy & ad choices. If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalised or limited ads, which still require some technical processing (for example the IP address) to deliver the ad and to prevent fraud.

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Buying Remove Ads turns off the interstitial ads.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases (gem packs, Starter Pack, Remove Ads) and the monthly Fate Pass Premium subscription are sold and processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your name, e-mail, payment card or billing address. The app only receives from Google Play the information that a product was bought (product ID, order ID and purchase token), so it can deliver the item, and checks whether the subscription is active. Google processes payment data as an independent controller under the Google Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel at any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

6. Legal bases

Delivering purchases is necessary to perform the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Personalised advertising is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. Delivering non-personalised ads and preventing ad fraud is based on legitimate interests in funding the free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

7. Sharing and transfers

We do not sell or share personal data – we do not have any. The only third parties that process data while you use the app are Google (AdMob, User Messaging Platform and Google Play). Google may process data outside the EEA, for example in the USA, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

9. Android permissions

Internet / network state – loading ads and connecting to Google Play; Google Play billing – purchases; Advertising ID (added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK) – advertising; Vibration – haptic feedback. The game does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

10. Children

Dice of Fate is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was processed by an ad provider, contact us and we will help.

11. Your choices and rights

Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads), and opt out of personalised ads there.

Ad consent: in the game under Settings › Privacy & ad choices (shown where required).

Deleting data: uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device. Data held by Google can be managed at myaccount.google.com.

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Slovakia the Office for Personal Data Protection (dataprotection.gov.sk). As we hold no personal data about you, requests concerning advertising or payment data are best directed to Google; we will gladly help: prvyucetjedna@gmail.com.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always published at this address with its effective date.

Zásady ochrany osobných údajov – Dice of Fate

Platné od: 26. novembra 2026

1. Kto sme

Dice of Fate (balík pre Android com.mrandhsdevs.diceoffate) je hra pre jedného hráča, ktorú vyvíja a vydáva MR and HS Devs, prevádzkovateľ podľa GDPR. Kontakt: prvyucetjedna@gmail.com.

2. Zhrnutie

Dice of Fate nemá používateľské účty ani vlastný server. My ako vývojár o vás nezbierame, nedostávame ani neukladáme žiadne osobné údaje. Postup v hre (hry, odomknutia, meny, nastavenia a záznamy o nákupoch) sa ukladá iba lokálne vo vašom zariadení. Bezplatná verzia zobrazuje reklamy Google AdMob a nákupy spracúva Google Play.

3. Reklama (Google AdMob)

Hra po niektorých dohraných hrách zobrazuje celoobrazovkové reklamy Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads SDK môže spracúvať reklamné ID, údaje o zariadení (model, verzia systému, jazyk), IP adresu a z nej odvodenú približnú polohu a interakcie s reklamami. V EHP, Spojenom kráľovstve a Švajčiarsku sa hra pred personalizáciou reklám pýta na súhlas cez Google User Messaging Platform; voľbu zmeníte v hre v Settings › Privacy & ad choices. Google je samostatným prevádzkovateľom (Zásady ochrany súkromia Google). Nákup Remove Ads vypne celoobrazovkové reklamy.

4. Nákupy (Google Play)

Nákupy v aplikácii a mesačné predplatné Fate Pass Premium predáva a spracúva výhradne Google Play. Nedostávame vaše meno, e-mail ani platobné údaje – len informáciu o zakúpenom produkte (ID produktu, objednávky a nákupný token), aby sme mohli položku doručiť. Predplatné sa automaticky obnovuje, kým ho nezrušíte v Google Play › Platby a predplatné › Predplatné.

5. Právne základy a prenosy

Doručenie nákupov – plnenie zmluvy (čl. 6 ods. 1 písm. b) GDPR); personalizované reklamy – súhlas (písm. a); nepersonalizované reklamy a ochrana pred podvodmi – oprávnený záujem (písm. f). Google môže údaje spracúvať aj mimo EHP (napr. v USA) na základe Rámca pre ochranu údajov EÚ – USA alebo štandardných zmluvných doložiek.

6. Deti

Hra nie je určená deťom do 13 rokov a vedome od detí nezbierame žiadne údaje.

7. Vaše práva a voľby

Reklamné ID môžete obnoviť alebo odstrániť v systéme Android v Nastavenia › Ochrana súkromia › Reklamy. Odinštalovaním aplikácie sa vymažú všetky herné údaje v zariadení. Máte právo na prístup, opravu, vymazanie, obmedzenie, prenosnosť, námietku, odvolanie súhlasu a právo podať sťažnosť Úradu na ochranu osobných údajov SR (dataprotection.gov.sk).

8. Zmeny

Zásady môžeme aktualizovať; aktuálna verzia je vždy na tejto adrese s dátumom platnosti.
